## Brand & Style

This design system channels the relentless intensity, precision, and drive of round-the-clock athletic performance. Designed for round-the-clock training culture, it targets dedicated athletes, late-night grinders, and goal-oriented fitness enthusiasts who demand directness, power, and absolute reliability.

The aesthetic fuses **High-Contrast Athletic** with modern technical dark minimalism:
- **Energy & Urgency:** Deep carbon obsidian foundations punctured by high-voltage athletic yellow highlights create an adrenaline-charged visual hierarchy.
- **Precision Engineering:** Clean architectural layouts, razor-sharp alignment, and structural accents evoking high-performance training facilities and equipment instrumentation.
- **Conversion Focus:** High-impact callouts, transparent membership architecture, and punchy, action-oriented microcopy engineered to eliminate friction and drive immediate commitment.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure visual stamina and extreme contrast, calibrated specifically for dark mode interfaces across late-night environments and bright mobile viewports.

- **Background Foundations:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#0E1115` (Deep canvas, immersive viewport backdrop)
  - Surface Void: `#14181F` (Cards, elevated panels, module shells)
  - Surface Highlight / Layer: `#1B2028` (Inputs, chip backgrounds, nested list containers)
- **Primary Energy:**
  - Kinetic Voltage Yellow: `#FFE500` (High-priority action triggers, active tabs, progress bars, key data highlights)
  - Electric Gold Accent: `#F5EE00` (Hover transitions, active state glows, badge highlights)
- **Typography & Structural Contrast:**
  - Pure Titanium: `#FFFFFF` (Impact headlines, key metric values, primary button labels on dark surfaces)
  - Slate Steel: `#94A3B8` (Body text, subtext, captions, structural dividers)
  - Carbon Border: `#28303C` (Subtle 1px perimeter definition on structural cards and input fields)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the raw power of athletic condensed typefaces with the clarity of a contemporary humanist geometric sans:

- **Display & Headings (Barlow Condensed):** Employs tight line-heights and assertive weights (700-800). All main headlines and numeric telemetry (e.g., pricing figures, 24/7 metrics) must be styled in uppercase or condensed title case for immediate visual impact.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for high legibility on dark surfaces. Generous x-height prevents eye fatigue when scanning training regimens, facility specs, or contract details.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Key tags and status markers leverage uppercase condensed tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`) to convey speed and mechanical precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined, fluid 12-column grid system calibrated to support high-density tactical dashboards alongside impactful, high-conversion acquisition landing flows.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer canvas margin and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers max out at `1280px` for optimal reading tracking and balanced peripheral focus.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Pricing modules transition from 3-column rows to 2-column or stacked arrangements.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` margin and `1rem` gutters. Complex data and horizontal feature tables collapse into vertical card chains with immediate edge-anchored bottom CTA trays.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Internal card padding and component distribution strictly adhere to modular `0.5rem` increments. Critical CTA clusters retain generous vertical clearances (`space-xl`) to prioritize focus.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft multi-point diffused dropshadows. Instead, elevation is created through tonal layering, crisp borders, and targeted luminescent energy:

- **Tonal Stepping:** 
  - Canvas: `#0E1115`
  - Base Cards & Sections: `#14181F` with a crisp `1px solid #28303C` border.
  - Hover / Active Interactive States: Shifts to `#1B2028` with border transition to `#FFE500`.
- **Luminescent Energy & Ambient Glows:**
  - High-priority elements (such as "Most Popular" pricing tiers or primary active triggers) utilize an energetic amber/yellow rim glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 229, 0, 0.18)`.
  - Floating sticky action buttons utilize a deep obsidian floor shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Dividers & Structural Ghost Lines:**
  - Section dividers and nested separators use a 1px line in `#28303C`, maintaining architectural discipline across dense technical layouts.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is grounded in athletic agility and mechanical structure. 

- **Primary Structure:** Structural elements (cards, input containers, modal viewports, segmented panels) apply a compact `0.25rem` (Soft) corner radius, presenting a deliberate, industrial aesthetic that resists overly rounded softness.
- **Status & Metric Badges:** Badges, facility tags (e.g., "OPEN 24 HOURS", "LIVE CAPACITY"), and micro-chips utilize pill-shaped styling (`rounded-full`) to contrast against the sharp architectural lines of the surrounding containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Call-to-Actions
- **Primary CTA:** Solid `#FFE500` fill, `#0E1115` bold uppercase Barlow Condensed text. Hover state shifts background to `#F5EE00` with subtle kinetic translation (`translate-y: -1px`) and an active amber glow. Primary buttons include supportive subtext/microcopy below the main label (e.g., "JOIN IN 60 SECONDS • NO CONTRACT").
- **Secondary CTA:** Obsidian `#14181F` background, `1px solid #28303C` border, `#FFFFFF` text. On hover, the border illuminates to `#FFE500`.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, `#94A3B8` text with underline or arrow indicator, transitioning to `#FFE500` on hover.

### Pricing Cards
- **Architecture:** Surface colored `#14181F` wrapped in a sharp `1px solid #28303C` border.
- **Featured / Tier-Highlight:** Promoted memberships feature an illuminated perimeter (`1px solid #FFE500`) paired with the ambient yellow glow.
- **Data Display:** Prices displayed in Barlow Condensed 72px bold white text with the billing period in muted `#94A3B8` Plus Jakarta Sans. Includes an unstyled bulleted checklist with custom `#FFE500` check icons.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status Badges (Pill):** Compact containers with an ultra-subtle tint (`rgba(255, 229, 0, 0.1)`), an energetic yellow border (`1px solid rgba(255, 229, 0, 0.3)`), and high-contrast `#FFE500` label text.
- **Facility / Filter Chips:** Muted `#1B2028` background with slate `#94A3B8` text, switching to solid `#FFE500` with black text when selected.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Recessed `#0E1115` base fill, `1px solid #28303C` border, `#FFFFFF` text. On focus: border sharpens to `#FFE500` with an immediate zero-blur outline ring (`2px solid rgba(255, 229, 0, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square/circular controls with `#14181F` fill and `#28303C` borders. Checked state triggers solid `#FFE500` fill with obsidian `#0E1115` check marks.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Facility Status & Telemetry Rows:** Divided by hairline borders (`#28303C`), featuring real-time gym occupancy metrics, current club access logs, and 24-hour equipment availability stats set in condensed typography.
